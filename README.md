No Outpost Attacks: Capturing or burning outposts is strictly forbidden and usually triggers an immediate alliance war.
No Village Stealing: Taking or attacking resource villages in the outer kingdoms (Ice, Desert, Peaks) is never allowed.
±10 Level & Might Point Matching: Players below level 70 should only attack within a ±10 level range. Above level 70, base your target selection on Might Point categories (e.g., within 1–3 million Might Points) to avoid farming weaker players.
Sabotage & Double Attacks (Punishment Exceptions): Sabotaging and double-attacking (gang-attacking a target) are banned in normal play, but both can be used as official punishments against rule-breakers.
No Open Gates Under Punishment: If you broke an unwritten rule and are receiving retaliation as a penalty, you are not allowed to open your gates—you must stand and defend.
Level 70 Food & Mead Restrictions: A level 70 player with mead production must not attack a level 70 player without mead production using max-food troops combined with relic commanders.
No Neutral Player Attacks: Attacking alliances or players with Non-Aggression Pacts (NAP) or neutral standings outside of active war is prohibited.
Level 70 Might Point Brackets: Above level 70, you are only allowed to attack players who fall within your same Might Point bracket:
0.5M – 5M Might Points
5M – 10M Might Points
10M – 15M Might Points
15M+ Might Points
You can attack higher might gap players but if you do they can attack you back
Level Brackets (Below Level 70): Players under level 70 are restricted to attacking only within their specific level range:
Level 10 – 25
Level 25 – 35
Level 35 – 50
Level 50 – 69
Fresh Level 70 Exception: A freshly reached Level 70 player who does NOT yet have mead production is allowed to attack Level 60–69 players, BUT is strictly forbidden from using rewarded mead troops (units that consume mead, obtained via events or rewards) against them.
